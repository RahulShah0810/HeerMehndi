# Heer Mehndi — Brand Theme & Logo Design Guide

This document contains the complete visual theme, color codes, typography system, cultural motif guidelines, and design principles extracted from the **Heer Mehndi** website. Use this reference when creating logos, brand assets, social graphics, or marketing collateral for Master Artist Hirva.

---

## 💎 Brand Identity Summary

- **Brand Name**: Heer Mehndi
- **Founder & Master Artist**: Hirva

## 🔤 Indian Traditional Royal Typography System

The website uses a curated combination of Google Fonts carefully selected to project **authentic Indian royal calligraphy and heritage** while keeping all copy strictly in English.

### 1. Primary Royal Calligraphic Font (Logo & Crest Typography)
- **Font Name**: `Rozha One` (Google Font)
- **Character**: Majestic Indian-inspired high-contrast serif font derived from traditional Indian editorial lettering and royal signboards.
- **Usage**: Primary Logo ("Heer Mehndi"), royal badges, and main headline accents.
- **CSS Rule**: `font-family: 'Rozha One', 'Cinzel', serif;`

### 2. Imperial Heritage Serif Font (Section Headings)
- **Font Name**: `Marcellus` & `Cinzel`
- **Character**: Trajan-style imperial serif font used by luxury couture houses and royal Indian palaces.
- **Usage**: Section titles (*Occasions, Portfolio Showcase, About Hirva, Reserve Your Experience*).
- **CSS Rule**: `font-family: 'Marcellus', 'Cinzel', serif;`

### 3. Delicate Italic Accent Font (Subtitles & Vows)
- **Font Name**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Italic)
- **Character**: Flowing, poetic italic serif for romantic bridal accents.
- **Usage**: Taglines like *"Organic Henna Artistry"*, artist signature subtitles.
- **CSS Rule**: `font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif;`

### 4. Supporting English Body Font (Clean Modern Legibility)
- **Font Name**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Character**: Clean, geometric, neutral modern sans-serif for crisp English body paragraphs and button labels.
- **CSS Rule**: `font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif;`

## 🌿 Art Motifs & Cultural Design Elements for Logo

When designing logos or badge icons for Heer Mehndi, incorporate or draw inspiration from these 4 core motifs:

1. **The Signature Gold Accent Dot**:
   - A single circular solid dot (`#C5A059`) at the end of the logotype: `Heer Mehndi •`
   - Represents the *Bindi* / sacred mark of completion and perfection in Indian culture.

2. **Vintage Zardozi Framing**:
   - Fine hairline gold stroke arches (`#C5A059` at 1px stroke) or dashed circular rings (`#1E352B` with `stroke-dasharray="3 3"`).
   - Gives the logo a delicate royal crest aura without cluttering the clean text.

3. **Royal Mehndi Mandala & Lotus Symbolism**:
   - A central circular mandala featuring fine concentric dots, lotus petal outlines (`Kamal`), and 8-point radial symmetry.
   - Ideal for sub-marks, wax seals, stamps, or social media avatars.

4. **Paisley / Kalka Vine Curve**:
   - Organic flowing mango/paisley shape representing grace, fertility, and auspicious beginnings in South Asian heritage.
