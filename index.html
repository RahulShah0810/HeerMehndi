Information & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details regarding booking timelines, organic stain care, destination travel, and custom bridal consults.

How far in advance should I book my luxury bridal henna? ↓

For peak destination wedding seasons (October to April), we recommend reserving your date 6 to 12 months in advance. Because Hirva accepts a limited number of high-profile commissions per month, early consultation ensures dedicated priority.

Is your henna paste 100% natural and safe for sensitive skin? ↓

Absolutely. We hand-mix fresh organic batches before every event using premium Sojat henna powder, lemon juice, sugar, and therapeutic-grade eucalyptus and lavender essential oils. We never use PPD, black henna, or synthetic chemicals.

How do I achieve the deepest mahogany stain for my wedding day? ↓

Bridal henna should be applied 48 hours prior to the main wedding ceremony. Keep the paste intact for 8-10 hours, seal with lemon-sugar syrup, and avoid direct water contact for the first 24 hours. Full aftercare balm instructions are provided upon completion.

Do you travel internationally for destination weddings? ↓

Yes, Hirva and her senior team travel worldwide across Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Asia. Destination booking packages include travel, private lounge artist teams, and complete wedding event coverage.

Can we incorporate custom personal elements into the bridal design? ↓

Yes! Custom bridal design consultations include sketching your personal story—incorporating initial hidden dates, proposal skylines, sacred mantras, pet portraits, or custom wedding logos seamlessly into the traditional motifs.
